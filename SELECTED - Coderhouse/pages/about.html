THE ART OF SELECTION

SELECTED is an independent platform dedicated to underground electronic culture. We discover and document artists, sounds, spaces and ideas that deserve to be heard.

WHY SELECTED

In a world where music is everywhere, finding something meaningful can be harder than ever. SELECTED was created to put human curation back at the centre of discovery. We believe that selection is an art. It is about listening deeply, looking beyond the obvious and creating connections between people, music and culture.

WHAT WE DO

SELECTED creates spaces for discovery through:

  • Audiovisual Sessions — live recordings with emerging and established selectors.
  • Conversations — interviews and stories behind the people making the music.
  • Listening Sessions — collective experiences built around listening.
  • Workshops — knowledge, experimentation and creative exchange.
  • Community Gatherings — bringing people together around music and culture.
  • Collaborations — projects with artists, labels and independent cultural spaces.

OUR APPROACH

We don't aim to show everything. We look for what matters.

From a forgotten record to an emerging artist, from a hidden room to a new sound — SELECTED exists to give attention to the things that might otherwise go unnoticed.

SOUND. PEOPLE. SPACE. CULTURE.

SELECTED is a living archive of electronic culture — built one selection at a time.
